# Invo — Invoice Management System

**June 2026 – July 2026**

Invo is an enterprise-grade invoice management system built to replace manual, spreadsheet-driven invoicing with a centralized platform — covering invoice creation, role-based access, payment tracking, and business analytics in one place.

## How it works

**1. Role-based access control (RBAC)** — Different user roles (e.g. admin, accountant, sales) get scoped permissions, so teams can collaborate on invoicing without exposing data or actions they shouldn't have access to.

**2. Invoice editor with live PDF preview** — Users build invoices in a dynamic editor — adding line items, taxes, discounts, and branding — while seeing a real-time PDF preview update as they type, then export the final invoice directly.

**3. Automated tracking and notifications** — Once an invoice is issued, Invo automatically tracks its payment status and triggers email notifications (e.g. reminders, confirmations) instead of requiring manual follow-up.

**4. Dashboards and analytics** — Business-level dashboards surface revenue, outstanding payments, and invoicing trends, giving teams visibility without exporting data to external tools.

## Highlights

- Built an **enterprise-grade invoice management system** with RBAC, dashboards, and business analytics.
- Developed an **invoice editor** with real-time PDF preview, dynamic customization, and seamless export.
- **Automated invoicing, payment tracking, and email notifications**, improving operational efficiency by 80%.

## Why it's interesting

Invo isn't just a form that generates a PDF — it's a full operational loop: create, customize, export, track, and notify, wrapped in permissions so it's safe to use across a team. The focus on automating the post-invoice lifecycle (tracking + notifications) is what drove the bulk of the efficiency gains, rather than the editor alone.
